# Inn mode

You are at an inn counter. The safest action is usually to confirm a stay to restore HP and MP.

- `stay` - confirm resting at the inn.
- `interact` - talk to the innkeeper or confirm a prompt.
- `cancel` - back out of a dialog.
- `wait` - let the rest animation and dialog finish.

First-pass limitation: cursor navigation is not implemented. Only use `stay` when the dialog is already asking for confirmation.
